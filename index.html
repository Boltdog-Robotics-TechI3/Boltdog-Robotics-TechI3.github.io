Boltdog Robotics

Who Are We?

Boltdog Robotics is a student led VEX robotics organization at Louisiana Tech University founded in 2022. Our team designs, builds, and programs robots to compete in competitions while developing real world technical skills. Beyond competition, we are committed to STEM outreach, working to inspire the next generation of engineers through hands on learning and community engagement.
